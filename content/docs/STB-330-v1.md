# STB-330 v1 — Vantrexa (ILM-330) Thawed-State Handling and Excursion Allowances

**Owner:** Analytical Development · **Status:** Effective · **Effective date:** 2025-09-01

## 1. Product
Vantrexa mRNA-LNP suspension, 10-dose vial. Long-term storage −25 to −15 °C. Shipped and held **thawed at 2–8 °C** for distribution; thawed shelf life 30 days.

## 2. Excursion allowances (thawed state)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Label range (thawed) | 2–8 °C |
| Stability-supported ceiling | 25 °C |
| Cumulative TOR budget | **12 h** at ≤ 25 °C, total across thawed life |
| MKT limit over the assessed window | **8 °C** |
| Refreezing | Not permitted |

## 3. Rationale
LNP particle size and mRNA integrity drift measurably after 12 h above 8 °C (study ILM-330-STB-02). The MKT limit equals the label maximum because the degradation is not Arrhenius-linear in this range; MKT is reported for completeness but offers no relief.
