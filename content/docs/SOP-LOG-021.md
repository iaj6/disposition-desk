# SOP-LOG-021 v3 — Carrier and Lane Management

**Owner:** Logistics · **Status:** Effective · **Effective date:** 2025-05-05

## 4. Tarmac exposure
4.1 Irrespective of carrier contract terms, any tarmac or ramp dwell exceeding **4 hours** shall be reported to QA the same day as a potential excursion, and the logger shall be reviewed on arrival. This threshold is deliberately stricter than the 8 h contracted with carriers.

## 6. Lane approval and review
6.1 A lane is approved by an effective Lane Risk Assessment (LRA-).

6.2 An LRA shall be reviewed at least every **24 months** (SOP-QA-001 §4) and upon any change to carrier, packout or the product's stability profile.

6.3 **A lane whose LRA is overdue for review, or whose LRA cites a superseded qualification report or stability profile, is not considered approved** until the LRA is re-issued. Shipments already in transit on such a lane are assessed under SOP-QA-014 with the lane status noted in the deviation.
