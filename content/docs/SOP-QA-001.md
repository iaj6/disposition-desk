# SOP-QA-001 v6 — Document Hierarchy and Control

**Owner:** Quality Assurance · **Status:** Effective · **Effective date:** 2025-11-01

## 1. Purpose
Defines the hierarchy of controlled documents at Ilmenau Therapeutics GmbH and the rule applied when two documents disagree.

## 2. Hierarchy
1. Quality Policy (QP-)
2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP-)
3. Work Instructions (WI-)
4. Forms, templates and registers (FRM-, REG-)

Technical registers such as the Stability Register (STB-), Qualification Reports (QR-) and Lane Risk Assessments (LRA-) are **evidence documents**. They do not sit in the procedural hierarchy; they are cited by it.

## 3. Conflict rule
3.1 Where a Work Instruction conflicts with an SOP, the SOP governs and the WI shall be revised within 30 days of the conflict being identified.

3.2 Where an internal document conflicts with an applicable regulatory requirement (EU GDP 2013/C 343/01, USP <1079>, WHO TRS 961 Annex 9), the **stricter** requirement governs pending revision.

3.3 Where two versions of the same document exist, only the version with status *Effective* governs. Superseded versions are retained for traceability and shall not be used for decisions.

3.4 Where a derived document (for example a Lane Risk Assessment) quotes a value from an evidence document (for example a Qualification Report), the **evidence document** is authoritative. A discrepancy between them is a documentation deviation and shall be raised against the derived document.

## 4. Review periodicity
Lane Risk Assessments shall be reviewed at least every 24 months or upon change of carrier, packout or product stability profile, whichever is first.
