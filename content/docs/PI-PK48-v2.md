# PI-PK48 v2 — Packing Instruction: PK-48 Kestrel Passive 48 L

**Owner:** Warehouse · **Status:** Effective · **Effective date:** 2025-07-14

1. Pre-condition four 9 L gel bricks for a minimum of **48 h at 5 °C** (verify with the conditioning log).
2. Place two bricks in the base, load product, place two bricks on top. Do not use dry ice with this shipper.
3. **Fill the payload space to at least 60 %** using product cartons or approved void-fill cartons (item VF-48). Record the fill percentage on the packing list; the 96 h summer hold time does not apply below 60 % (QR-PK48-2025-011 §4.1).
4. Start the logger, place it on top of the product, close and seal.
