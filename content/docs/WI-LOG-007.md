# WI-LOG-007 v2 — Use of Mean Kinetic Temperature at Receiving

**Owner:** Logistics · **Status:** Effective · **Effective date:** 2025-02-01

## 1. Purpose
Gives receiving sites a quick check so that minor excursions do not hold up putaway.

## 2. Instruction
2.1 On receipt of a shipment with an alarm, download the logger and compute MKT over the full shipment window using the Frostwerk LoggerView tool.

2.2 **If MKT is within the product's MKT limit and no single reading exceeded the ceiling, the receiving site may release the shipment to unrestricted stock and record a note in the receiving log. No QA deviation is required.**

2.3 If MKT exceeds the limit, place the shipment on hold and notify QA.

## 3. Note
This instruction was written against SOP-QA-014 v3 and has not been revised since SOP-QA-014 v4 introduced the repeat-excursion rule.
