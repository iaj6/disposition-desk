# STB-201 v3 — Kestrelin (ILM-201) Stability Summary and Excursion Allowances

**Owner:** Analytical Development · **Status:** Effective · **Effective date:** 2026-05-15 · **Supersedes:** STB-201 v2

## 1. Product
Kestrelimab 150 mg/mL solution for injection in pre-filled syringe. Label storage: 2–8 °C, do not freeze, protect from light.

## 2. Supporting studies
- ILM-201-STB-05: 36-month real-time at 5 °C (complete).
- ILM-201-STB-07: excursion study — 48 h at 30 °C, 72 h at 25 °C, three freeze-thaw cycles (complete, report dated 2026-03-30).

## 3. Excursion allowances (this version governs)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Label range | 2–8 °C |
| Stability-supported ceiling | **30 °C** |
| Cumulative time out of refrigeration (TOR) budget | **48 h** at ≤ 30 °C, across the product's life |
| MKT limit over the assessed window | 25 °C |
| ΔH for MKT | 83.144 kJ/mol (default) |
| Freezing | Not supported. Any reading below 0 °C → reject. |

## 4. Change from v2
Study ILM-201-STB-07 extended the supported ceiling from 25 °C to 30 °C and the cumulative budget from 24 h to 48 h. All downstream documents citing the 24 h figure are to be updated (change control CC-2026-031, open).
