# NF-TC-2025 — Nordfracht Air Cargo: Pharma Service Terms and Conditions

**Status:** External (carrier document, accepted 2025-01-20) · **Owner:** Procurement

## Clause 7 — Temperature-sensitive cargo
7.1 Nordfracht will handle cargo booked under the PharmaPlus service in accordance with IATA CEIV Pharma.

7.2 **Tarmac exposure** at origin and destination will not exceed **8 hours** per leg. Exposure beyond 8 hours entitles the shipper to a service credit; it does not constitute an admission of product impact.

7.3 Temperature-controlled ULD storage is available at FRA, LEJ and SIN. **At BOS it is provided on a best-efforts basis only.**

7.4 Nordfracht does not open, inspect or re-ice passive shippers.
