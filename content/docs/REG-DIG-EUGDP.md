# REG-DIG-EUGDP v1 — Regulatory Digest: EU Guidelines on Good Distribution Practice (2013/C 343/01), Chapter 9 Transportation

**Owner:** Regulatory Affairs · **Status:** Effective · **Effective date:** 2024-06-01

## Key points
- 9.2: Required storage conditions shall be maintained during transportation within the defined limits as described by the manufacturer or on the outer packaging.
- 9.2: Where a deviation such as a temperature excursion has occurred during transportation, this shall be reported to the distributor and the recipient. A procedure shall be in place for investigating and handling temperature excursions.
- 9.3: Risk assessment of delivery routes should be used to determine where temperature controls are required. Equipment used for temperature monitoring should be maintained and calibrated.
- 9.4: Passive containers: validated to ensure the required conditions are maintained for the expected duration; the qualification should reflect the actual route conditions (seasonal profiles).
