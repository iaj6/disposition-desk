# QR-PKA12-2024-002 — Operational Qualification of Active Container PK-A12 (ThermoVault A12)

**Owner:** Packaging Engineering · **Status:** Effective · **Issued:** 2024-11-05 · **Valid until:** 2026-11-05

## Summary
ThermoVault A12 active (compressor) container, 2–8 °C set point.

| Parameter | Result |
|---|---|
| Battery autonomy at 35 °C ambient | 72 h |
| Battery autonomy at 43 °C ambient | 48 h |
| Recovery after 20 min door-open at 30 °C | 14 min to ≤ 8 °C |
| Alarm | Audible + telemetry on > 8 °C for 15 min, battery < 20 % |

## Conditions
Container must be plugged in at any ground handling stop exceeding 6 h. Telemetry SIM must be active; unit ships with 90-day roaming plan.
