# QR-PK48-2025-011 — Thermal Qualification of Packout PK-48 (Frostwerk Kestrel Passive 48 L)

**Owner:** Packaging Engineering · **Status:** Effective · **Issued:** 2025-06-30 · **Valid until:** 2027-06-30 · **Supersedes:** QR-PK48-2023-004

## 1. Configuration tested
Frostwerk Kestrel 48 L VIP shipper, 2 × 9 L gel bricks top and bottom, pre-conditioned 48 h at 5 °C, payload of Kestrelin PFS cartons.

## 2. Ambient profiles
- **Summer:** ISTA 7E summer profile extended with the Ilmenau FRA–BOS composite (4 h at 35 °C tarmac segment inserted at hour 30).
- **Winter:** ISTA 7E winter profile.

## 3. Results — qualified hold time (payload 2–8 °C)

| Profile | Payload fill | Qualified hold time |
|---|---|---|
| Summer | ≥ 60 % | **96 h** |
| Summer | 30–59 % | 72 h |
| Winter | ≥ 60 % | 120 h |

## 4. Conditions of validity
4.1 The 96 h summer hold time applies **only at ≥ 60 % payload fill**. Under-filled shippers lose thermal mass and were qualified to 72 h.

4.2 Pre-conditioning of 48 h at 5 °C is mandatory. Bricks conditioned for less than 36 h were not tested.

## 5. Change from QR-PK48-2023-004
The 2023 report qualified 120 h in summer against the plain ISTA 7E profile. The added FRA–BOS tarmac segment reduced the demonstrated hold time to 96 h. **Documents that cite 120 h for summer lanes are out of date.**
